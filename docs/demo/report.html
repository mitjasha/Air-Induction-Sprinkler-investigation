Interpretation

The momentum root and liquid balances verify implementation consistency. They do not establish device accuracy. Air flow is referenced to ambient pressure and temperature. CU describes a single head over the modeled disk and is sensitive to binning; it is not the uniformity of a sprinkler installation.

Limitations

  • Uncalibrated closures; no device-specific validation available
  • Single-head annular CU depends on quadrature/binning; not field overlap CU
  • Homogeneous outlet closure neglects phase slip and internal gas-compression work. Hydraulic-to-kinetic power is an admissibility bound, not a full thermodynamic balance.
  • Horizontal radial film; prescribed lossless redirection at the edge. No wind, evaporation, droplet deformation, coalescence, or secondary breakup.

Research provenance

The software includes a separate audit of the 2019 similarity-criterion paper and flow accounting from the 2021 control-system paper. The unresolved equation/table discrepancy is documented in the repository. Neither source is used to claim validation of this demonstration. Direct breakup in aerated operation requires checking the applicability of the film model.
